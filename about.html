Our Vision, Mission & Values

In an era where online content is saturated with distractions that fragment readers’ attention spans, we look to collaborate with well-crafted IPs—those built on rich worldviews, strong backstories, and vivid character interactions. These are IPs that reward readers who invest time and emotional attention, and we believe these bonds deepen meaningfully over time.

Our core focus—leveraging technological advancements in merchandising to create high-quality products—aligns naturally with such IPs. By building offline touchpoints, we offer new pathways for audiences to form emotional connections with the IP, guiding them back to the stories that creators have carefully crafted. We have extensive experience balancing regular product lines, limited editions, exclusive collaborations, and seasonal drops in the region.

With a proven track record of maintaining brand integrity while driving consumer demand, we support IPs in expanding their market presence and elevating brand equity across Greater China and Southeast Asia. Our goal is to partner with IP creators on a scalable, region-specific strategy that balances exclusivity and accessibility, ensuring sustained resonance and long-term profitability.
